# Product Guidelines

## Tone and Voice
- **Professional & Efficient:** The system should use clear, direct, and unambiguous language. Communication should prioritize productivity and data clarity, helping gym owners make quick, informed decisions.

## Visual Identity & UX
- **Data-Rich Dashboards:** The user interface must prioritize numerical data and financial summaries.
- **Real-Time Financial Focus:** Dashboards should emphasize daily-updated totals for membership payments and service fees, providing an immediate view of the current month's accumulated revenue.
- **Historical Comparison:** While the current month view "resets" for focus, the system must provide easy access to previous months' performance metrics.

## Design Principles
- **Data Persistence:** All financial transactions must be logged daily to the database to support historical reporting and audit trails.
- **Clarity over Decoration:** Avoid unnecessary visual flourishes that do not contribute to data interpretation.
- **Actionable Insights:** Ensure that critical information, such as expiring memberships or pending payments, is highlighted and easily accessible.
