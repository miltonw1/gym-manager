# Gym Manager

## Initial Concept
A multi-tenant Gym Management System designed for gym owners to manage members, memberships, and custom workout routines. The system focuses on tracking member statuses and providing financial insights.

## Product Definition

### Target Audience
- **Gym Owners and Administrators:** The primary users who manage the business operations of a single or multiple gym locations within the multi-tenant platform.

### Core Value Proposition
- **Efficient Membership Management:** Automated tracking of membership statuses (active, expiring, expired) to ensure timely renewals and revenue retention.
- **Customized Training Experience:** Tools for creating and assigning tailored workout routines from a library of exercises, providing value to both the gym and its members.
- **Financial Clarity:** Monthly financial tracking and reporting to monitor income and business growth.

### Key Features
- **Multi-tenant Architecture:** Securely host multiple gym organizations on a single platform.
- **Member Management:** Comprehensive profiles for gym members, including history and contact information.
- **Flexible Memberships:** Support for multiple active memberships per member with automated expiration alerts.
- **Routine Builder:** A library of exercises to create custom routines that can be assigned digitally or printed for physical use.
- **Financial Tracking:** Monthly reporting on income generated from memberships and other services.
